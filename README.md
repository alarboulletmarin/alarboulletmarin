# Hi, I'm Andréa

Applied-AI software engineer in Nice, France, and a runner. On the side I build small, fast apps that respect your time and your data: no account, no tracking, and offline whenever possible. TypeScript for the web, Rust for the desktop. Most projects speak English and French.

**Feedback is the most useful thing you can give me.** Try one of these, then open an issue and tell me what's broken, missing or confusing. You can also find me on X: [@andddrealm](https://x.com/andddrealm).
